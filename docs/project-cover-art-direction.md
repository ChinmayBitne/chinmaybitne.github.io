# Project cover art direction

The nine covers were generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool and optimized to WebP for this portfolio.

## Shared direction

- Premium editorial still-life or handcrafted physical diorama
- Wide landscape composition suitable for a 16:10 website crop
- Tactile, believable materials and strong photographic lighting
- Warm ivory, ink black, cobalt, coral, yellow, and project-specific supporting colors
- No readable text, logos, watermarks, humanoid robots, brains, neon cyberpunk, generic glowing networks, or floating glass dashboards
- Human design-studio art direction with a clear physical metaphor for the engineering work

## Final prompt set

### AnswerLeaf AI

Tactile cream course papers, translucent citation tabs, a cobalt binder, coral pencil, and a clean answer card connected to three source sheets by red thread on a warm studio tabletop. Communicate scholarship, traceability, and trustworthy synthesis.

### ASTRA AI

An amber ceramic assistant orb surrounded by precisely arranged objects for files, calendar, screen, microphone, and automation, connected through slim mechanical linkages on a charcoal architect's worktable.

### NYC Airbnb Market Intelligence

A miniature Manhattan block made from cream data cards and paper buildings, placed over a translucent turquoise map grid with coral location pins, a brass key, and colored price and neighborhood tokens.

### Alternovation

A furniture concept transitioning from graphite sketch and measurement blocks into a refined plywood-and-wool chair prototype, surrounded by material swatches, a brass ruler, and colored cost tokens.

### NutriAssist AI

A balanced meal photographed overhead with translucent analysis frames, a camera-lens object, colored nutrient beads, a paper meal journal, and red thread connecting the food to a reference card.

### LDWS & Object Detection

A driver-eye view on a dry Southwest highway, with lane and object detection reinterpreted as restrained physical drafting tape, translucent safety-coral geometry, and analog technical collage.

### Emotional Tone & Readability

Colored paper ribbons emerging from a vintage black microphone, changing texture to represent emotional signals and resolving into neatly sorted cards on a muted lilac studio set.

### Disease Risk & Readmission

Five restrained frosted-acrylic clinical abstraction tiles connected by red patient-journey thread, with brass probability weights arranged on a clean diagnostic grid.

### Connected Weather Station

A handcrafted Arizona desert weather-station diorama with sensor mast, translucent cloud forms, cobalt rain gauge, sun disc, wind ribbons, and stacked data tiles leading to a compact forecast-model object.
